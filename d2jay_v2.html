About Me

WHO
I AM

"I grew up obsessed with light. Now I get paid to control it."

I'm Jeremiah — a filmmaker, photographer, and creative director based in the DC/Maryland/Virginia area. I've been shooting since my teens, when I turned my parents' garage into a production space and started charging local artists for music videos and recording sessions.

I studied film and television at SCAD — one of the country's top art and design schools — and I've been working in the DMV and Atlanta markets ever since. Whether it's a music video concept that needs to live up to a single you spent months on, a wedding day that only happens once, or a listing that needs to move fast — I treat every project like it's the most important one on my schedule. Because to my clients, it usually is.

I don't have a studio. That's intentional. Your music video belongs in the locations that match your sound. Your wedding belongs in your venue. I bring the gear. You bring the vision. We make something real.

Ask Me Directly
How much does a music video cost?
Music video packages start at $750 for a performance-based, single-location shoot. A standard story-driven production is $1,500. A full cinematic multi-location production runs $2,500 to $3,500. Drone, extra shoot days, and color grading upgrades are available as add-ons. I'll always give you a clear quote before anything is agreed to.
I have a low budget. Is there still something we can do?
Yes. The $750 starter package exists specifically for independent artists who want a real video without a label budget. I've seen what can be done with one location and a focused concept. Reach out, tell me what you're working with, and we'll build something that fits.
What does wedding coverage include?
Photo-only starts at $2,200. Video-only starts at $2,400. A combined photo and film package starts at $3,800. All packages include full-day coverage, a complete digital gallery, a cinematic highlight film, and all commercial usage rights. Engagement sessions, drone footage, and a same-day reel are available as upgrades. I cover the whole day — getting ready through last dance — so you're not coordinating multiple vendors.
How do I know the quality will be there?
Watch the work. Every project in the portfolio is something I shot and edited. I shoot in 4K cinema quality, color grade every project by hand, and don't deliver until I'm satisfied with it. I also send you a preview before final delivery so you have input on the final cut.
Do you work outside the DC/Maryland/Virginia area?
Yes. I'm based in the DMV but I travel for the right projects. I have strong ties to Atlanta and work there regularly. For projects outside the DMV, travel costs are added to the package. Message me with your location and what you need and we'll figure out if it makes sense.
How far in advance do I need to book?
Wedding dates book 6 to 12 months out, especially for May through October. Music video and photography shoots can often happen within 2 to 4 weeks. If your date is soon, still reach out — if I'm available, I'll make it work. A deposit secures the date.
What do I need to bring to a shoot?
Just yourself and your ideas. I bring all camera equipment, lighting, audio, drone gear, and stabilizers. For music videos, we'll work through a creative treatment together before the shoot day so we arrive with a shot list and a plan. You focus on performing — I'll handle the rest.
How long does it take to get my finished video or photos?
Photography galleries are typically delivered within 5 to 7 business days. Music video edits run 1 to 2 weeks depending on scope. Wedding films take 4 to 6 weeks. Rush turnaround is available on request. I'll give you a specific timeline when we confirm the booking.

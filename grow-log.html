Grow these first

POTG x Lilac Diesel (Ethos), Grease Truck & Brain Storm (Dirty Bird), Ocean Fruit (Square One), and Glazed Pastries (In House) — the best mix of real grow reports, credible potency, and forgiving cultivation.

Highest claimed THC

Cherry Poppers (31%, Barney's Farm) and Birthday Cake (24–27%, BlimBurn) test high on paper — but these are unverified breeder marketing numbers, not lab-confirmed.

Thin on data

Mind Flayer, Gas Nana, Sunset Skunk, Frozen Papaya, Divine Truffle, and the unconfirmed Acapulco Gold pack have little to no firsthand grow data. Treat as pheno-hunts.

Red flags

Frozen Papaya's only documented run went intersex mid-flower. Mind Flayer threw male flowers in one grow. Cull aggressively if you see anthers.

Methodology & Caveats

Read before you trust a THC number
  • THC numbers are mostly unverified. Outside a few well-characterized genotypes, percentages come from breeder and seed-shop marketing, which routinely inflates figures. Even GG4 — one of cannabis's most tested strains — is listed anywhere from 20% (Leafly) to 32% (AllBud) depending on the source.
  • Small-batch drops have thin data by nature. Roughly half of this collection has few or no completed firsthand grow/smoke reports. Absence of reviews isn't evidence of low quality — it just means less certainty going in.
  • AI-generated "strain guide" content was excluded. Several seed-shop blogs publish fabricated statistics. Sentiment here comes from GrowDiaries diaries, independent smoke reports, Rollitup/THCFarmer threads, and Leafly/AllBud/Zamnesia user reviews.
  • Phenotype variability is the rule, not the exception — especially for F1s and reversed/S1 lines. Expect to grow multiple seeds of the same strain and select for the keeper.
  • Cull anthers immediately. Two strains in this collection (Frozen Papaya, Mind Flayer) showed intersex traits in their only documented grows. Hermie risk outweighs any terpene appeal.
  • Buy from verified vendors. Counterfeit seed packs are a documented risk, particularly flagged here for the Thug Pug line.

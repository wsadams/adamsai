Foundation
CORE POSITIONING
Jay's market position is not videographer, not photographer, not DJ. It is the SCAD-trained creative operator who bundles all of it — and comes to you with full production capability.

The DMV creative services market is bifurcated: large agencies ($5K–$50K projects) on one end, commodity solo operators on the other. Jay lives in the high-value middle — trained at an elite art institution, multi-disciplined, personally invested, and operationally lean. No studio overhead means competitive pricing and professional results.

The Positioning Statement

D2Jay Creative

A SCAD-trained visual storyteller bringing cinematic video production, photography, music production, and DJ services directly to independent artists, couples, and brands in the DMV — with no studio fees and no creative compromises.

One-Sentence Pitch

"I bring the vision, the gear, and the expertise — directly to you."

The "I come to you" model is implied, not stated. The real differentiator is the breadth of SCAD-trained skill in a single operator that competitors don't have.

The Four Business Lanes (in priority order)

  • Video Production — Music videos (indie artists), wedding videography, real estate drone video, brand/promo content. Primary revenue driver. Highest price-per-project and clearest portfolio path.
  • Photography — Events, weddings, portraits, real estate, brand. Natural cross-sell with video. Adds recurring revenue from real estate relationships.
  • Music Production & Engineering — Beat leases, exclusives, mixing, mastering. Secondary service for artist clients who already hired Jay for video. Not lead-with, but a valuable upsell.
  • DJ Services — Events, weddings, corporate. Listed but not featured prominently. Real income stream but not the identity.
Strategic Verdict

Solitude Lighting is its own separate brand. Do not merge it with D2Jay Creative. Keep them distinct — mention Solitude Lighting on the D2Jay site as a separate offering with a link, nothing more.

The Unfair Advantages Jay Has Right Now

Credential

SCAD Cum Laude — Savannah College of Art and Design is a nationally recognized institution. This is the trump card almost no DMV competitor has.

Range

Video + Photo + Audio + DJ — Clients who need multiple services want one trusted operator. Jay is the only one who can credibly offer all four.

Rootedness

PG County native — For DMV artists and couples, having a local who knows the culture and community matters. That's a trust signal agencies can't fake.

Go-To-Market
MARKETING PLAN
Jay doesn't need a big ad budget. He needs a systematic approach to referrals, partnerships, and content — the three channels that work for solo creatives with limited time and money.

CHANNEL 1 — REFERRAL ENGINE

Referrals are the highest-converting lead source for creative services. One satisfied artist client tells their label group. One wedding couple tells their engaged friends. One realtor talks to their brokerage of 40 agents.

The Referral Playbook

  • After every delivered project, send a personal thank-you + request for a Google review. Make it dead simple — include the direct Google review link.
  • Offer a referral discount: $100–$150 off their next booking when they refer someone who books. Tell them explicitly at delivery.
  • Keep a "relationships list" of every past client. Text them on their anniversary, album drop day, or home listing day. Stay top of mind.

CHANNEL 2 — STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS

Realtors

Target 2 PG County realtors in month one. Offer a free listing shoot as a trial. If they love the result, they become 2–3 shoots per month permanently. Pitch the brokerage office with a one-pager, not just the individual agent.

Wedding Venues

Venues refer creatives to every couple that books. Getting on even one preferred vendor list can mean 4–8 wedding inquiries per year from a single relationship. Target: Bolger Center, Martin's Crosswinds, Harmony Hall, The Kentlands Mansion.

Recording Studios

Local recording studios (Black Violin Music Studio, DMV independent spots) regularly have artists finishing tracks who need videos. Partner with 2 studios to be their referred videographer — offer a referral commission.

Music Promoters

DMV music showcases, open mics, and promoters have constant artist relationships. Attend 2 events per month. Have a physical card or QR code to your site. Be a presence before you need bookings.

Event Planners

One DMV event planner relationship can mean DJ + photo bookings year-round. They want reliability above everything. Show up on time, deliver files fast, and you're their person for life.

Local Barbershops / Hair Salons

In PG County, barbershops are community hubs for artists. Leave cards. Better: shoot content for the shop itself (offer to do it for a post mention) and let the owner introduce you to their artist clientele.

CHANNEL 3 — CONTENT MARKETING

Every shoot is a content asset. Every content asset is a referral machine that works 24/7 without Jay being present. The goal: within 6 months, someone searching Google for a music video director in Maryland finds Jay's site, Jay's YouTube, Jay's Instagram, and Jay's TikTok on the first page.

Blog Content Template

Post title format: "[Genre] Music Video Shoot in [City, MD] — Behind the Scenes with [Artist/D2Jay]"

Content: 300–500 words, 5–8 photos, embedded YouTube BTS video. Link to booking page. Takes 30 minutes to write. Ranks for 10–20 long-tail terms automatically.

Paid Ads (Month 3+)

Once portfolio is built: $200–$300/month in targeted Instagram ads to specific ZIP codes (Upper Marlboro, Bowie, Mitchellville, Largo) targeting engaged couples and local artists. Don't run ads before the portfolio is ready — traffic without proof is wasted money.

Strategic Priority
D2JAY VS. SOLITUDE LIGHTING
This is the most important strategic question in the brief. Jay has two distinct businesses — and limited time. Where he invests first determines what becomes primary income.

Jay should pour his primary energy into D2Jay Creative first — not because Solitude Lighting is weaker, but because D2Jay has faster inbound potential, lower startup cost, and a more direct path from first client to recurring revenue in the near term.

Strategic Assessment — May 2026
D2JAY CREATIVE — The Case

Why D2Jay Wins Right Now

  • Zero inventory cost. Jay already has his gear. Portfolio is buildable immediately — even spec shoots cost nothing but time.
  • Inbound discovery is faster. People search Google and Instagram for videographers every day. Lighting installation has a longer sales cycle — it requires homeowners with renovation intent.
  • Higher per-job revenue in fewer hours. A $1,500 music video takes 6–8 hours total. A $1,500 lighting install takes 3–4 visits plus sourcing, planning, and follow-up.
  • Personal brand compounds. Every D2Jay post, every Google review, and every blog post builds an asset that keeps generating leads for years.
SOLITUDE LIGHTING — The Case

When Solitude Should Scale

Solitude Lighting is not the wrong business — it's the wrong business to lead with right now. Here's when the calculus changes:

  • When D2Jay Creative is generating $3,000+/month and Jay has consistent time to invest in a second business
  • When Jay has a helper or subcontractor who can take installations without him being on-site
  • When Solitude's website, SEO, and Google Business are fully built (currently unclear if these exist)
  • Solitude's exterior lighting category has very high ticket sizes ($2,000–$8,000 installs) — that's compelling at scale

How They Coexist (Right Now)

BusinessJay's Energy AllocationRole
D2Jay Creative 70–80% of entrepreneurship effort Primary income engine · Personal brand · Daily content
Solitude Lighting 20–30% — take jobs when they come, don't chase yet Supplemental income · Separate brand · Reinvest when D2Jay stable
The One-Line Answer

Pour time into D2Jay Creative. Let Solitude Lighting take incoming jobs without active marketing spend for now. When D2Jay is stable, build Solitude into what it should be: a premium home lighting brand in its own right — not a side service attached to a videography business.

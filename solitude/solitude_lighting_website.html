CRI (Color Rendering Index) measures how accurately a light source shows true color compared to natural sunlight. CRI 90 means your food looks appetizing, your countertops look right, and your kitchen feels alive. Cheap LEDs at CRI 70 make everything look flat and washed out. We only install CRI 90+. It's non-negotiable.

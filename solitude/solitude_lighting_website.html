Questions

Good Questions
Deserve Real Answers

How long does a typical installation take?

+

Most interior installs — including full kitchen lighting — are completed in a single day. Event setups and holiday displays take 2–4 hours depending on size. We confirm timing during your free consultation so you know exactly what to expect.

Do I need to be an electrician to operate the lights after install?

+

Not at all. Before we leave, we walk you through the app and set up your favorite scenes. Everything is designed to be simple — one tap changes the entire mood of your room. It's honestly more fun than it is technical.

Are your LEDs smart home compatible? Alexa, Google Home?

+

Yes. Our lighting systems integrate with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. You can also control everything from the dedicated app or a physical remote if you prefer. We match the technology to what works best for your household.

Will installation damage my cabinets or walls?

+

No. We use professional-grade mounting methods that are designed to be clean and low-impact. We treat every home like our own. If you're ever concerned about a specific surface, we discuss it during the consultation and find the right approach before we touch anything.

What areas do you serve?

+

We serve the full DMV area — Washington DC, Maryland (including Prince George's County, Montgomery County, Howard County, Anne Arundel County), and Northern Virginia. Not sure if you're in range? Just reach out and we'll confirm.

Is the consultation really free? What does it include?

+

Yes, completely free — no strings attached. We come to your space, take measurements, talk through your vision, and give you a detailed written quote within 24 hours. You decide from there with zero pressure.

What's the difference between CRI 90 and regular LEDs?

+

CRI stands for Color Rendering Index — it measures how accurately a light source shows the true color of objects compared to natural sunlight. CRI 90 means colors look rich, food looks appetizing, and your kitchen surfaces look like they're supposed to. Cheap LEDs (CRI 70 or below) make everything look flat and washed out. We only use CRI 90 or higher — it's not optional.

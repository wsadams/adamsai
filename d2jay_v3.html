Ask Me Directly
How much does a music video cost?
Music video packages start at $750 for a performance-based single-location shoot. A standard story-driven production is $1,500. A full cinematic multi-location production runs $2,500 to $3,500. Drone, extra shoot days, and color grading upgrades are available as add-ons. I'll always give you a clear quote before anything is agreed to.
I have a limited budget. Can we still work together?
Yes. The $750 starter package exists specifically for independent artists who want a real video without a label budget. Tell me what you're working with and we'll build something that fits.
What does wedding coverage include?
Photo-only starts at $2,200. Video-only starts at $2,400. A combined photo and film package starts at $3,800. All packages include full-day coverage, a complete digital gallery, a cinematic highlight film, and all usage rights. Engagement sessions, drone, and same-day reels are available as upgrades.
How do I know the quality will be there?
Watch the work. Every project in the portfolio is something I shot and edited myself. I shoot in 4K, color grade every project by hand, and don't deliver until I'm satisfied. You also get a preview before final delivery.
Do you work outside DC, Maryland, and Virginia?
Yes. I'm based in the DMV and serve the area locally. I travel for the right projects — including Atlanta, where I work regularly. Travel costs are added for projects outside the DMV. Message me with your location and I'll let you know quickly.
How far in advance should I book?
Wedding dates typically book 6 to 12 months out, especially May through October. Music video and photo shoots can often happen within 2 to 4 weeks. If your date is soon, still reach out — if I'm available, I'll make it work.
What do I need to bring to a shoot?
Just yourself and your ideas. I bring all camera equipment, lighting, audio, drone gear, and stabilizers. For music videos, we'll work through a treatment together beforehand so we arrive on set with a clear plan.
How long until I get my finished photos or video?
Photography galleries are delivered within 5 to 7 business days. Music video edits take 1 to 2 weeks. Wedding films take 4 to 6 weeks. Rush turnaround is available on request. I'll give you a specific timeline when we confirm the booking.
